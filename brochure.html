Services & Expertise
— Who we are
Furniture design,
engineered.

No.Style is a design studio specialised in contemporary contract furniture. With 10 years of experience in the furniture industry as industrial designers, we are first and foremost technicians passionate about furniture.

Holding degrees in mechanical engineering, we master the technical constraints of industrialisation. As design-oriented engineers, we understand your needs and propose creative solutions aligned with your innovation strategy.

Our experience as designers at world-renowned studios in Milan — notably Claudio Bellini and Michele de Lucchi — allowed us to develop a refined style for modern, timeless furniture.
