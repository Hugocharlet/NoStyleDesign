Who are we?
HMBD is a design agency specializing in the creation of contemporary contract furniture. We offer a range of services including 3D modeling, visualization, mock-ups, and plans, all without copyright restrictions. We support you through the strategic phases of product development, from the initial idea to modeling, visualization renderings, and manufacturing plans. We aim to build a long-term partnership based on trust and complete transparency. That's why we offer service-based billing, and all developed models remain your sole property. We operate under confidentiality agreements to ensure the security of your confidential data, such as your know-how and models under development.

Expertise:
With 10 years of experience in the furniture sector as an industrial designer, Hugo Charlet is first and foremost a technician with a passion for furniture. A mechanical engineering graduate, he has a thorough understanding of the technical constraints related to industrialization. This design-driven engineer will understand your needs and propose creative solutions tailored to your innovation strategy. His experience as a designer with world-renowned agencies in Milan, including Claudio Bellini and Michele de Lucchi, allowed him to hone his style to create furniture with modern and timeless lines. Hugo Charlet is also a 3D trainer (Rhinoceros/Keyshot/Blender) and teaches materials technology at several architecture schools in Bordeaux. Passionate about new technologies, he is proficient in a wide range of software (Keyshot, Rhino, V-Ray, Blender among others) and stays at the forefront of the latest AI-driven design tools.
