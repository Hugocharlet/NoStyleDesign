WHO ARE WE?

No.Style is a design agency specialising in the creation of contemporary contract furniture. We offer a range of services including 3D modeling, visualization, mock-ups, and plans — all without copyright restrictions.

We support you through the strategic phases of product development, from the initial idea to modeling, visualization renderings, and manufacturing plans. We aim to build a long-term partnership based on trust and complete transparency.

Service-based billing. All developed models remain your sole property. We operate under confidentiality agreements to ensure the security of your know-how and models in development.

EXPERTISE

With 10 years of experience in the furniture sector as industrial designers, we are first and foremost technicians with a passion for furniture. 

Mechanical engineering graduates, we have a thorough understanding of the technical constraints related to industrialization. As design-driven engineers, we will understand your needs and propose creative solutions tailored to your innovation strategy.

Our experience as designers with world-renowned agencies in Milan, including Claudio Bellini and Michele de Lucchi, allowed us to hone our style to create furniture with modern and timeless lines.
